# Writing Style Rules

Applies to CVs, cover letters, and any follow-up messages drafted by
`/apply` or `/outcome`.

## Defaults (edit to match the candidate's actual voice from /setup)

- **Tone:** confident, direct, no false modesty — but no hype-speak either.
  Avoid "passionate," "results-driven," "synergy," "go-getter."
- **Sentence length:** short to medium. Avoid stacking three clauses in one
  bullet.
- **Voice:** active voice, past tense for past roles, present tense only
  for current role.
- **Bullets:** lead with the action and result, not the task. Prefer
  "Cut deploy time 40% by rebuilding the CI pipeline" over "Responsible for
  CI pipeline improvements."
- **US/Canada conventions:** no photo on the CV, no age/marital
  status/nationality, dates as Month YYYY, one page per ~8-10 years of
  experience is a reasonable target (see `05-cv-templates.md` for hard
  limits).

## Cover letters

- 3-4 short paragraphs, one page max: why this role, why this company
  specifically (not generic), a concrete example of relevant work, a short
  close. No restating the whole resume.
- Never open with "I am writing to apply for..." — start with something
  that shows you actually read the posting.

## Do's and don'ts

- Do quantify wherever the candidate has given a number.
- Do mirror the posting's terminology when the candidate genuinely has
  that skill (helps both ATS and human readers).
- Don't pad with skills the candidate hasn't confirmed.
- Don't use the same opening line across multiple applications — always
  tailor to the specific company.
