---
description: Build a prep pack for a scheduled interview
---

Prepare the candidate for an upcoming interview on a tracked application.

## Steps

1. Ask which application this is for (company + role), and find the
   archived materials under `documents/applications/<company>_<role>/`
   (posting, CV, cover letter, any notes from earlier interview rounds).
2. Ask what stage this interview is (phone screen, technical, behavioral,
   panel, final) — tailor the prep pack accordingly.
3. Research the company and, if named, the interviewers, using web search.
   Verify claims before including them (e.g. don't assert a company's
   values or recent news without a source) — a wrong guess is worse than
   no guess.
4. Map likely questions for this stage to the candidate's real experience,
   using their STAR examples from `07-interview-prep.md`. Don't invent
   experience — if there's a gap between what's likely to be asked and
   what the candidate has actually done, say so and help them find the
   closest honest angle.
5. Offer a mock interview: ask questions one at a time, give feedback on
   each answer, and only move to the next after the candidate responds.
6. Save any new STAR examples or feedback that came up back to
   `07-interview-prep.md` for future rounds.
