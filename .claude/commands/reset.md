---
description: Wipe profile data or the documents folder and start over
---

Ask the candidate what they want to reset:

1. **Profile only** — clear `CLAUDE.md` back to its unconfigured state and
   clear `01-candidate-profile.md` / `02-behavioral-profile.md`. Leaves
   `documents/` and the application tracker untouched.
2. **Documents folder only** — clear out `documents/` (keep the profile).
3. **Everything** — both of the above, plus optionally
   `job_search_tracker.csv` (ask separately; application history is often
   worth keeping even on a full profile reset).

Confirm exactly what will be deleted before doing it — this is destructive
and not undoable.
