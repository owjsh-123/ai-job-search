# documents/

Drop your source material here before running /setup — Claude will read
it instead of asking you to retype everything.

- cv/ — Your current CV as PDF or .tex
- linkedin/ — LinkedIn "Download your data" export (PDF or ZIP)
- diplomas/ — Degree certificates / transcripts (optional)
- references/ — Reference letters (optional)
- applications/ — Auto-populated by /apply, one folder per application, with the posting, CV, cover letter, and outcome notes

Nothing here is required — /setup will just ask more questions directly
if a folder is empty. Re-running /setup after adding more material is
safe; it won't duplicate what's already in your profile.
